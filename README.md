Ember Vault — a complete top-down stealth game in JavaScript/HTML5. You are Wren, a relic thief slipping through three floors of a lamplit vault at night: sneak past guards with raycast vision cones, hide in bushes, throw pebbles to pull patrols away, take every ember shard, and reach the green exit unseen. Guards patrol, pause and scan, grow suspicious, raise the alarm and chase; breaking line of sight lets you slip away and try a quieter route. Synthesized Web Audio footsteps, alerts and chimes — no audio files, no network requests, just static files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser
```

WASD / arrows to move, Shift to sneak, click to throw a pebble, P to pause, M to mute, R to restart the floor, Enter to confirm on menus.
